What I do

I grow the traffic you do not pay for, the right way

Organic search is the traffic you earn instead of rent. Done well, it becomes an asset that keeps returning customers long after the work is done, unlike paid ads that stop the moment you stop spending. That is the channel I build.

But I am not a "do more SEO" consultant. The industry is full of people who will sell you ten blog posts a month, a pile of backlinks, and a keyword list, none of which touch the actual reason your growth has stalled. I work the other way around. I find the one constraint that is capping growth, then fix it in the order that makes every next step pay off.

In practice that means I look at three things: whether the demand even exists and you are visible to it, whether people trust you enough to choose you, and whether it is easy for them to act once they do. Growth stalls at whichever of those is weakest. Fix the right one and the whole system moves.

Who I help

Built on healthcare, useful far beyond it

Most of my deepest work is in healthcare: multi-location clinics, private practices, allergy and immunology, primary care. Healthcare is a brutal test of organic growth because the stakes are real, the trust bar is high, and a single search often decides a new patient. If the method works there, it works.

That said, the method itself is industry-agnostic. It applies to any business where customers search before they buy and where outranking a competitor changes the month: professional services, local service businesses, ecommerce, and SaaS included. The constraint thinking does not care about the industry; only the specifics of the fix change.

  • Local service businesses competing for "near me" demand against bigger names.
  • Founders and owners who are tired of paying for SEO that never moves a real number.

How I think

Growth has one binding constraint at a time

This is the belief everything I do rests on. A system grows only as fast as its tightest bottleneck allows. You can publish more, build more links, and chase more keywords, and still flatline, because none of it touches the one thing actually holding you back.

So I refuse to start with volume. I start with the diagnosis: which of demand, trust, or conversion is the real limit right now. Then I sequence the fixes so the foundation can carry what comes next, instead of pouring effort into a leaking bucket. It feels slower at first. It is far faster at producing customers.

What I will not do
  • Sell you content or links that no customer is searching for.
  • Promise page one in a few weeks. Real growth, especially in healthcare, compounds over months.
  • Report vanity metrics. I measure what maps to revenue: calls, bookings, and customers, not just impressions.
  • Keep you dependent. If the honest answer is that your foundation is fine and you need patience, I will tell you.
